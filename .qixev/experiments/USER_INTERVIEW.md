# Structured Technician Interview Script

This script accompanies **Experiment #001**. Use it during or immediately following hands-on observation with practicing repair technicians.

---

## Part 1: Current Operational Workflow

1. **Job Tracking**:
   - *"How do you currently track repair jobs that come into your shop from intake to delivery?"*
   - *(Listen for: paper notebook, WhatsApp messages, whiteboard, sticky notes, Excel, specialized POS)*
2. **Customer Information**:
   - *"How do you keep track of which customer owns which device, and how to contact them when it is ready?"*
3. **Parts & Inventory Tracking**:
   - *"How do you track spare parts like screens, batteries, charging ports, or ICs?"*
   - *"How do you know whether you have a specific part in stock right now without physically opening your parts drawer?"*
4. **Historical Lookups**:
   - *"If a customer walks in asking about a repair you did two months ago, how do you find that record?"*
   - *"What happens when a customer brings back a device saying the same problem occurred again?"*

---

## Part 2: Friction & Operational Pain Points

1. **Time Waste**:
   - *"What part of your current job-tracking or parts process wastes the most time every day?"*
2. **Recurring Mistakes**:
   - *"What mistakes happen most often in the shop? (e.g. lost device details, forgotten passwords, wrong part ordered, undercharged labor)"*
3. **Information Loss**:
   - *"What information do you frequently lose, forget to write down, or struggle to find when you need it?"*
4. **Desired Improvements**:
   - *"If you could wave a magic wand and fix one annoying thing about how your shop runs its jobs, what would it be?"*

---

## Part 3: FieldKit Walkthrough & Exploration

Hand the technician the app (`v1.0.0`) and prompt them to perform the core workflow:

1. **First Impression**:
   - *"Looking at this initial screen, what do you think this application is designed to do?"*
2. **Intake Flow**:
   - *"Show me how you would record today's first customer repair."*
   - *(Observe silently without pointing)*
3. **Parts Allocation**:
   - *"Show me how you would record that a \$30 screen from your stock was used on that phone."*
4. **Job Search & History**:
   - *"Show me how you would find that job if you needed to check the total cost or warranty status."*
5. **Critical Feedback**:
   - *"What felt confusing, clunky, or took longer than it should have?"*
   - *"What on these screens feels completely unnecessary or distracting for a real repair shop?"*

---

## Part 4: Adoption & Practical Value

1. **Daily Utility**:
   - *"Would you actually use FieldKit tomorrow instead of your current notebook, spreadsheet, or chat workflow?"*
   - *"Why or why not?"*
2. **Adoption Barriers**:
   - *"What is the single biggest thing that would have to change or be added before you could comfortably use this every day?"*
3. **Offline & Data Privacy**:
   - *"FieldKit stores everything locally on your phone with zero internet access or cloud sync. Does that make you feel safer about your data, or do you worry about losing it if your phone breaks?"*
   - *(Explore local backup discoverability)*

---

## Part 5: Commercial & Pricing Signals

> [!CAUTION]
> **Strict Prerequisite**:
> Only ask these questions if the technician has established that FieldKit provides real, tangible value that could replace or improve their workflow. If they reject the core workflow, skip this section.

1. **Willingness to Pay**:
   - *"If this app reliably solved your job and parts tracking problem every day, would you pay money for it?"*
2. **Pricing Model**:
   - *"Would you expect to buy an app like this once, or pay an ongoing monthly or annual subscription?"*
3. **Value Threshold**:
   - *"What would make a paid version completely worthwhile for your shop?"*
   - **Crucial Rule**: *Never suggest a price figure (\$5, \$10, \$50). Wait for them to name their expectation first.*
